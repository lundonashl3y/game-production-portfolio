## Role
Project Manager.

## Platform
itch.io

## Team
Team: 4 contributors (Programming, Art, Design, Production)

## Project Overview

A young boy gets thirsty in the night. However, he forgot to clean his room. How will he navigate the mess? Use the lights throughout his room to help light his way.


## Production Challenge

The Midnight Glass was developed during a limited production timeframe, requiring the team to balance creative goals with deadlines. The primary challenge was time management — prioritizing features, coordinating tasks, and maintaining communication across the team to ensure the project could be completed and shipped.

## Responsibilities

- Coordinated communication between developers, artists, and designers.
- Tracked milestones, tasks, and production goals.
- Supported development planning and release preparation.
- Helped maintain team alignment throughout the development cycle.

## Production Highlights

- Completed and shipped as an indie game project on itch.io.
- Managed collaboration across a cross-functional development team.
- Supported the team through production planning, iteration, and final delivery.
