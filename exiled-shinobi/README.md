## Project Overview

Exiled Shinobi is a fast-paced action platformer inspired by games such as *Dead Cells*. Players take control of an exiled shinobi seeking to reclaim his honor by defeating the lord of all samurai.

The game focuses on high-mobility combat, sword techniques, dash mechanics, and navigating challenging castle environments filled with enemies and obstacles.

## Production Challenges

Developing a movement-focused action game required coordination between programming, level design, art, and audio teams to create a cohesive gameplay experience.

Key production challenges included:

- Coordinating gameplay systems between designers and programmers.
- Supporting level design and combat progression.
- Maintaining communication between creative and technical teams.
- Balancing feature development within a limited production timeline.
- Supporting iteration through testing and feedback.
