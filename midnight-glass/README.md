## Project Overview

The Midnight Glass is a puzzle adventure game where players guide a young boy through a dark bedroom by activating lights to safely reach a glass of water. The project was developed for Games For Love Cause Jam and released on itch.io.

**Play the Game**

https://greatsnow.itch.io/the-midnight-glass

## Production Challenges

Developed within a limited game jam schedule, the team needed to balance creative ideas with strict production deadlines. Coordinating work across programming, art, and design required consistent communication to keep development moving toward a successful release.
