## Project Overview

Dice and Dragons is a strategy auto-battler experience where players support a wizard exploring dangerous caves filled with powerful dragons.

Combat is determined through dice rolls, while players influence the adventure by selecting upgrade cards to improve the hero's abilities and prepare for future battles.

Players can also change camera perspectives throughout the experience to observe the wizard's journey.
